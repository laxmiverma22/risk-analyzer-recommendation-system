# Project Statement

## Problem Statement
Students may face academic difficulties because of low marks, poor attendance , backlogs, irregular study habits, or inadequate exam preparation. It can be difficult to identify the risk factors early.
This project provides a simple Python-based system that analyzes student information, calculates an academic risk score, identifies risk factors, and provide srecommendations for improvement.

## Scope
The system focuses on analyzing academic and study-related factors provided by the student. It classifies the student into Low Risk, Medium Risk, or High Risk and provides recommendations based on the identified risk factors.

## Target Users 
- Student
- Teachers
- Academic mentors

## High-Level Features
- Student data collection 
- Data validation
- Risk score calculation
- Risk level classification
- Risk factor identification
- Recommendation generation
- Student risk report
